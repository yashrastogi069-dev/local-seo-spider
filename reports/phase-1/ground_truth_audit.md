## 1. Executive Summary

This audit establishes the provenance, independence, integrity, and lack of circularity of the ground truth used across all evaluation benchmarks in Local SEO Spider.

To ensure benchmark trustworthiness:
1. **Ground truth questions and answers are strictly independent** of crawler outputs and model predictions.
2. **Zero query generation shortcuts** exist: no test case was generated by running retrieval first and writing questions to match retrieved snippets.
3. **Target facts are grounded directly** in the immutable corpus fixture `FROZEN_PAGE_RECORDS`.
4. **All 155 test queries** are uniquely keyed by stable permanent identifiers (`DEV-01`–`40`, `CAL-01`–`25`, `BLIND-01`–`40`, `ADV-01`–`30`, `REG-01`–`20`).

## 3. Ground Truth Verification & Non-Circularity Audit

Every test case in `BENCHMARK_CASES` was independently audited against the source documents to verify:
1. **Target Fact Verifiability**:
   - The string or entity designated in `target_fact` must be explicitly present in the body text or structured JSON of the corresponding `relevant_urls`.
   - Example: For case `DEV-02`, query `"What is the price for iPhone 9?"`, the target fact `"549"` is verified against `FROZEN_PAGE_RECORDS[15]["extracted_text"]` containing `"price: 549"`.
2. **Relevant URL Precision**:
   - `relevant_urls` contains only URLs that directly supply evidence for the question.
   - For adversarial/unanswerable out-of-domain questions (`relevant_urls: []`), no page in the corpus contains the requested information.
   - For conflict questions (e.g. `CONF-01`), `relevant_urls` points to both the active policy (`/refund-policy`) and the contradictory archive (`/archive/refund-policy`).
3. **Zero Circular Generation**:
   - Neither LLMs nor local extraction pipelines were used to synthesize ground truth labels from corpus text.
   - No benchmark ground truth was modified to accommodate retrieval or planner failure.
